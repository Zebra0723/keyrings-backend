The Finding Joy series

Under Taliban rule, a girl named Laleh learns tennis in secret — a game that could cost her everything. She escapes across borders and fear alike, carrying her sister, her family, and one battered racket toward the professional stage. Told in the first person, it is a story of courage, exile, and the sport that becomes both weapon and refuge.

What it's about

courage, family, exile, belonging

courage

A forbidden game, played anyway — and the cost of wanting something that badly.

family

exile

Borders crossed and left behind, and the ache of belonging to two places and neither.

belonging

A sport that becomes a home — both weapon and refuge on the road to the professional stage.
