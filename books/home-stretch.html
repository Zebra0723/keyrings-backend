Book 3 of the Finding Joy series

home stretch

Two girls. One sport. The last set.

Coming Soon

Two girls who learned the game a world apart now stand on the same side of the net — and, sometimes, across it. Everything Laleh has fought for comes down to a final season, a final rivalry, and a friendship that has to survive both.

In the trilogy's closing book, the sport that once meant survival becomes something harder to name: a home. home stretch brings the Finding Joy series to its finish line — a story about belonging, letting go, and the last set that decides who you become.
