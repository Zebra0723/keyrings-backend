Book 2 of the Finding Joy series

recovering connection

One letter. Fifteen years. And the only name I knew was Laleh.

Coming Soon

A single letter arrives, and fifteen years fold in on themselves. It carries only one name — Laleh — and a question she has spent half her life refusing to ask: who did she leave behind, and what did it cost to survive?

Between the pressure of the tour and the quiet of an unfamiliar home, she begins to trace the threads that link her past to her present. recovering connection, the second book in the Finding Joy series, is a story about memory, family scattered by exile, and the courage it takes to reach back toward what was lost.
