Book 1 of the Finding Joy series

finding joy

It's more than a game. It's where I find joy.

Coming Soon

In Kabul, tennis is forbidden to girls. Yousafi plays anyway — before dawn, behind walls, with a borrowed racket and the sound of her own heartbeat for a crowd. What begins as a stolen secret becomes the one thing worth risking everything for.

When the danger closes in, Yousafi and her family are forced to run. Carrying little more than her mother's medallion and a battered racket, she chases an impossible dream across borders, courts, and continents. finding joy is the first book in a trilogy about courage, family, and the game that gives a girl a reason to keep fighting.
