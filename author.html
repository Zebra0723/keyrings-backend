arjun jain

Arjun Jain is the author of the Finding Joy series, a young-adult trilogy following Yousafi — a girl who learns tennis in secret under Taliban rule and fights her way, across borders and against the odds, toward the professional stage.

Written in the first person, the series draws its heart from themes of courage, family, exile, and belonging, and from the idea that a single sport can be both a weapon and a refuge. This placeholder biography will be replaced with a full author bio ahead of the first book's release.
